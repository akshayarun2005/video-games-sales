01 · Problem statement

Raw sales tables hide the story

The video game market spans many genres, platforms and regional audiences. A raw table makes it hard to see which categories and platforms perform strongly, how regional preferences differ, and how the sales of leading titles vary across release years.

Project objective

Use exploratory data analysis and visualizations to compare global and regional sales by genre, platform and release year, and communicate the resulting patterns clearly.

Scope: descriptive analysis only. No machine-learning prediction is required.

02 · Key uses

What the analysis lets you do

  • AIdentify the genres with the highest combined sales among the selected titles.
  • BCompare platforms by the combined global sales represented in the dataset.
  • CUnderstand the contribution of North America, Europe, Japan and other regions.
  • DExplore release-year patterns among the top-selling titles.
  • ECompare genre and platform combinations using a heatmap.
  • FSupport introductory market research and data-driven discussion.

    08 · Limitations & data notes

    What these results can and can't tell you

    Sample vs the whole market

    The project analysis covers the 100 best-selling titles, so it describes blockbusters. Switch the dashboard to "All games" to see how the conclusions change across every record.

    Units, not revenue

    Sales are in millions of units sold. Price differences between platforms and eras mean units are not the same as money earned.

    Estimates and gaps

    VGChartz figures are estimates, not audited publisher data. Coverage thins after 2016 (344 titles in 2016, 3 in 2017). 271 games have no release year and 58 have no publisher, so they are left out of those charts.

    Descriptive, not causal

    The analysis shows what happened, not why. For example, lower totals in recent years may reflect incomplete data or newer titles having had less time to sell.

    About the numbers on this page: every figure is computed directly from the project's CSV (vgsales.csv, 16,598 records) with pandas, and the top-100 results match the charts in the original presentation. The original Matplotlib charts are one click away under each top-100 chart.

    09 · Conclusion

    What we learned

    • The project demonstrates a complete beginner-level data analysis workflow.
    • Sales can be compared across genres, platforms, regions and release years using grouped summaries.
    • Visualizations make patterns in the selected records easier to interpret than raw tables.
    • The output includes a cleaned CSV file and reusable charts.
    • Results are descriptive and limited to the included top-100 sample.
    • Comparing the sample with the full dataset shows how much blockbuster titles shape the picture.
